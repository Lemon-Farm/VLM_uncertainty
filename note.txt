Top-2 logit gap
For the Bayesian-LoRA top-2 logits, define the mean logit gap as
gap = mu_1 - mu_2, where mu_1 and mu_2 are the predictive means of the
first- and second-ranked tokens. Smaller gap values mean the top-1 and
top-2 candidates are closer and the top-1 prediction is less stable.
